## The name

*Orbis* is Latin for "circle" and "world"; *orbis terrarum* — "the circle of lands", the whole known world.
The ring in our mark holds day and night, and where they meet it is always sunrise.

The name is written **Orbios**. The software at the heart of it is **OrbiOS**.
